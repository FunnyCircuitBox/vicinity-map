    FAQ

    Questions

    Can I buy $VICINITY now?

    No. It hasn't launched. No presale, no whitelist, no airdrop. If someone offers you one, it's a scam. Use the checker above.

    Is connecting my wallet safe?

    Connecting only shares your public address. Verifying asks you to sign a plain message, which can't move funds. Never approve a transaction or share your recovery phrase on any site you don't fully trust.

    Do I need crypto to use the map?

    No. The map will be free. A wallet is only needed if you want community rewards later.

    What location data do you keep?

    Only the name of your city. Your exact position is used once to find it, then discarded.

    Who is behind this?

    A small independent builder. All code is public on GitHub so you can check it yourself.

    Will the price go up?

    Nobody honest can tell you that. Most meme coins lose nearly all their value. Join for the community and the fun, never with money you can't afford to lose.

    Risk disclosure

    Meme coins are highly speculative, and most lose all or nearly all their value. Nothing on this site is financial, legal or tax advice, or a promise of profit. Crypto transactions can't be reversed. Always double-check addresses, and never share your wallet's secret phrase with anyone.
