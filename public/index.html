    FAQ

    Questions

    What is Vicinity?

    A community map where every city gets its own page for local memes, check-ins and weekly votes, powered by the $VICINITY token. Next comes the Vicinity Launchpad, where every city can launch its own coin.

    How do I become a Founding Supporter?

    Hold $VICINITY. That's it. Supporters are picked by an on-chain snapshot, so there's nothing to sign up for. You can check your status anytime with "Verify" above.

    Is this a rug pull?

    No. Minting and freezing are switched off, liquidity is burned when it graduates on pump.fun, and every team wallet is listed here. You can check all of it live on the blockchain.

    Which contract address is real?

    Only the one shown on this page and in the official token list. Paste any address into the checker to confirm.

    Is verifying my wallet safe?

    Yes. You only sign a plain message, which can't move funds, and verifying doesn't save your address.

    How do I claim my city?

    Hold at least 1,000,000 $VICINITY, be in the city, and sign one free message. One wallet can claim one city, and each city has one founder. Claiming costs nothing.

    What does a City Founder get?

    Your wallet shown as the founder on the city page, and first right to launch your city's coin when the Vicinity Launchpad opens. Claims are re-checked at the Launchpad snapshot, so keep holding 1,000,000+ $VICINITY.

    Two cities have the same name. Which one do I get?

    The one you pick. Every city has its own ID number, and the ID is what gets claimed, never just the name. Search results show the state and country so you can tell them apart. For city coins, the biggest city with a name keeps the plain ticker (London, UK is $LONDON) and the others add their country or state code (London, Canada is $LONDONCA).

    Can someone use a VPN or fake GPS to claim another city?

    We make it hard. Your phone's GPS must be inside the city, and your internet connection must be in the same country and nearby. VPNs, proxies, cloud servers and Tor are blocked. No online check is perfect, so country moderators can flag suspicious claims, and every founder is re-checked at the Launchpad snapshot.

    Who moderates a country's city coins?

    The city founder in that country who holds the most $VICINITY, checked live on the blockchain. Team wallets can't be moderators. The final moderator is fixed at the Launchpad snapshot.

    My city isn't listed. What now?

    Use "Add it" in the city search. You need to be there, and the new city is claimed by you.

    What location data do you keep?

    None from you. Your location is used once to check you're inside the city, then thrown away. A city you add gets a center point rounded to about 10 km.
