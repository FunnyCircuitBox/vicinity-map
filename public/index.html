Vicinity Map is a community map where every city gets its own page. Check in, post local memes and vote for the best one each week. Cities rank by how active their people are, not by who holds the most coins.

Roadmap

  1. Phase 1 — Vicinity Map (building now): city pages, check-ins, local memes, weekly votes.
  2. Phase 2 — Vicinity Passport: collectible stamps for visiting local places. Starts only after Phase 1 has real users.
  3. Phase 3 — Vicinity Launchpad: only after an independent legal review. Not promised.

Our rules

  • We only store your city, never your exact location.
  • No fake users, fake counters or paid fake hype.
  • Every wallet the team controls will be published before any launch.
  • The code is open source, so anyone can check it.
