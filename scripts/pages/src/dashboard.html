              Roles & responsibilities

              Who does what, now and when Vicinity goes live.

              Every role except Admin is earned from live blockchain data. Fall below the line and the role moves to the next person on the next check.

              🏅

              Holder

              Holds any $VICINITY · vote ×1

              Now

              • Verify your wallet and set your home community.
              • Post memes, check-ins and discussions in your city and country.
              • Vote in the weekly meme vote. Report spam and scams.

              When Vicinity goes live

              • Your rank among all holders updates every minute.
              • Only holders can post and vote, so keep holding.
              • Holders at the snapshot become Founding Supporters: early access to city coins on the Launchpad.
              👑

              City Founder

              Claimed a city · 1,000,000+ · vote ×2

              Now

              • Get founder-ready: set your home community and plan to hold 1,000,000+.
              • Bring your locals in: every member makes the city's coin stronger.
              • Claims open the moment $VICINITY launches. First come, first served.

              When Vicinity goes live

              • Claim the city while standing inside its boundary.
              • Moderate the city's feed: review reports, hide spam.
              • Keep 1,000,000+ or lose the seat on the next check.
              • At the Launchpad: first right to launch the city's one official coin.
              🛡️

              Country Manager

              The founder holding the most in a country · vote ×3

              Now

              • The seat opens once the first cities are claimed. Until then the Admin covers it.
              • Become a City Founder first: the manager is chosen among founders.

              When Vicinity goes live

              • Approve or decline "add my town" requests in your country.
              • Hide posts anywhere in the country; ban scammers from the country's feeds.
              • Help review city coin launches in the country.
              • The seat moves live to whichever founder holds the most; it's fixed at the Launchpad snapshot.
              ⚙️

              Admin

              The Vicinity team · team wallets are public

              Now

              • Runs the map, approves new communities anywhere, and handles disputes.
              • Moderates every feed and can ban across all of Vicinity.
              • Publishes team wallets and announces the snapshot time.

              When Vicinity goes live

              • Publishes the only official contract address.
              • Runs the Launchpad and its schedule.
              • Releases claims that broke the rules (fake location, VPN).
