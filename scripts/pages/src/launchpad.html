Why a city launchpad

A coin with a place, people and rules.

  • No copycats. Each community gets exactly one official coin, and its ticker is reserved (London, UK is $LONDON; London, Canada is $LONDONCA).
  • Real holders. Locals are verified by wallet, location and one login per person.
  • Accountable leaders. Founders and managers keep their roles only while they hold, checked live.
  • Built for the long run. Every coin comes with its community's feed, check-ins and weekly votes.
